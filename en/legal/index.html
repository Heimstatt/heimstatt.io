Privacy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short

This site sets no cookies, measures nothing and loads nothing from third parties. We do not collect data about you unless you write to us.

Who is behind it

Freddie, reachable at hello@heimstatt.io. Heimstatt is a personal, non-commercial project.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver the site to you, GitHub technically sees your IP address. We do not receive any access data ourselves. More in GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you write to us

Mail to hello@heimstatt.io is forwarded by Forward Email (USA). We use your message only to reply and delete it once it is no longer needed for that.

Your data, your questions

You can ask at any time what we have stored about you and have it corrected or deleted. An email to hello@heimstatt.io is enough.
